Privacy Policy for Strata Driller

Last updated: 2026/09/02

Introduction

Strata Driller ("the App") is a single-player offline game developed by LDV Studios. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to your privacy — namely, that we don't collect anything from you.

Information We Collect

None. Strata Driller does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data. The game runs entirely on your device.

All game progress (upgrades, materials, save files) is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Strata Driller does not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data because we do not collect any data.

Children's Privacy

Strata Driller does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. If you have any questions, you can contact us at ldvstudio.dev@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy in the future. Any changes will be posted at this URL. Users will be notified of significant changes via the app's store listing.
